# Marketing Experiments

A working library of marketing experiments, observations and learnings across B2B growth, content, social, events and AI.

The goal is simple: document what I tested, what worked, what didn't, why I think it happened, and what I'd try next.

## What I'm exploring

- B2B organic social and content
- Growth marketing and conversion
- Event marketing and post-event follow-up
- Content distribution and repurposing
- AI-assisted marketing workflows
- SEO and organic growth

## Experiments

### 001 — Event-led LinkedIn content

Do event-day LinkedIn posts outperform typical organic brand content, and what might be driving the difference?

**What I explored:** Event marketing, organic social, B2B content, partner distribution and LinkedIn performance.

**Key finding:** Across the sample, event-day posts generated roughly 2.7× more impressions and 4.6× higher average engagement rate than non-event content — but simply announcing event attendance didn't show the same pattern.
