
# Marketing Experiments

A working library of marketing experiments, observations and learnings across B2B growth, content, social, events and marketing operations.

The goal is simple: explore marketing questions, test assumptions where possible, document what worked (and what didn't), understand why, and identify what I'd try next.

Some experiments are based on real campaign performance, while others document hypotheses and proposed approaches that haven't been tested yet.

## What I'm exploring

- B2B organic social and content
- Growth marketing and conversion
- Event marketing and post-event follow-up
- Content distribution and repurposing
- Account-based marketing (ABM) and lifecycle marketing
- AI-assisted marketing workflows and automation
- SEO and organic growth
- Marketing operations and process improvement

## Experiments

### 001 — Event-led LinkedIn content

Do event-day LinkedIn posts outperform typical organic brand content, and what might be driving the difference?

**What I explored:** Event marketing, organic social, B2B content, partner distribution and LinkedIn performance.

**Key finding:** Across the sample, event-day posts generated roughly 2.7× more impressions and 4.6× higher average engagement rate than non-event content — but simply announcing event attendance didn't show the same pattern.

→ [Read Experiment 001](experiments/001-event-led-linkedin-content.md)

### 002 — Actionable LinkedIn carousels

Do educational carousels actually drive stronger engagement, or is the topic and utility doing the heavy lifting?

**What I explored:** LinkedIn carousels, educational content, content utility, audience relevance and organic engagement.

**Key finding:** Three actionable carousels averaged a 30.8% engagement rate, but individual performance ranged from 9.7% to 50.26%. The strongest-performing post also had the lowest reach, suggesting that useful carousel content may drive deeper engagement without necessarily increasing distribution.

→ [Read Experiment 002](experiments/002-actionable-linkedin-carousels.md)

### 003 — Lifecycle-based strategic gifting

Can occasion-based strategic gifting create more meaningful engagement across the B2B customer lifecycle than a standard, one-size-fits-all merchandise approach?

**What I'm exploring:** Account-based marketing, B2B lifecycle marketing, strategic gifting, relationship engagement and conversion.

**Working hypothesis:** Gifting tied to meaningful moments in the customer relationship may generate stronger engagement than generic promotional merchandise. The experiment explores how gifting could support acquisition, conversion and retention, with different success metrics for each stage.

**Status:** Planning — Not Yet Launched. No results available yet.
