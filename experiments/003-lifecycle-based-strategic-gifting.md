
# Experiment 003: Lifecycle-Based Strategic Gifting

**Area:** Account-Based Marketing (ABM) & B2B Lifecycle Marketing  
**Channel:** Strategic Gifting / Direct Outreach  
**Status:** Planning — Not Yet Launched

## Context

Traditional promotional merchandise often focuses on visibility and brand awareness. But what if gifting could play a more intentional role throughout the B2B customer journey?

This project explores an occasion-based gifting framework designed to support three stages of the customer lifecycle:

- **Acquire:** Create opportunities to connect with prospective customers and priority accounts.
- **Convert:** Strengthen relationships with prospects during meaningful stages of the sales process.
- **Retain:** Recognise customer milestones, celebrate partnerships and encourage long-term relationships.

Rather than treating merchandise as a standalone giveaway, the approach explores how thoughtful gifting at relevant moments could support broader marketing and relationship objectives.

## Question

Can occasion-based strategic gifting create more meaningful engagement across the B2B customer lifecycle than a standard, one-size-fits-all merchandise approach?

## Status

This experiment is currently in the planning stage. The gifting framework, measurement approach and results will be documented as the initiative develops.
