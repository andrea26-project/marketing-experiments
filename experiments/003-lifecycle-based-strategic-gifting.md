
# Experiment 003: Lifecycle-Based Strategic Gifting

**Area:** Account-Based Marketing (ABM) & B2B Lifecycle Marketing  
**Channel:** Strategic Gifting / Direct Outreach  
**Status:** Planning — Not Yet Launched

## Context

Traditional promotional merchandise often focuses on visibility and brand awareness. But what if gifting could play a more intentional role throughout the B2B customer journey?

This project explores an occasion-based gifting framework designed to support three stages of the customer lifecycle:

- **Acquire:** Create opportunities to connect with prospective customers and priority accounts.
- **Convert:** Strengthen relationships with prospects during meaningful stages of the sales process.
- **Retain:** Recognise customer milestones, celebrate partnerships and encourage long-term relationships.

Rather than treating merchandise as a standalone giveaway, the approach explores how thoughtful gifting at relevant moments could support broader marketing and relationship objectives.

## Question

Can occasion-based strategic gifting create more meaningful engagement across the B2B customer lifecycle than a standard, one-size-fits-all merchandise approach?

## Status

This experiment is currently in the planning stage. The gifting framework, measurement approach and results will be documented as the initiative develops.


## Hypothesis

Strategic gifting could be more effective when the gift is connected to a meaningful moment in the customer relationship, rather than distributed as generic promotional merchandise.

This hypothesis is based on three ideas:

1. **Relevance creates stronger connections.** A gift that reflects the recipient's relationship with the business or a specific milestone may feel more thoughtful and memorable than a standard branded giveaway.

2. **Timing creates opportunities for engagement.** Gifting at the right moment, such as an initial introduction, a product demonstration, a successful go-live or a customer anniversary, may create natural opportunities for conversations and follow-up.

3. **Different lifecycle stages require different objectives.** A prospecting gift might help initiate a conversation, while a customer milestone gift could reinforce an existing relationship. Success should therefore be measured differently at each stage.

The working hypothesis is that an occasion-based gifting approach can improve relationship engagement and support acquisition, conversion and retention objectives.

However, gifting alone cannot be assumed to generate pipeline or improve retention. These outcomes will need to be evaluated alongside other sales and customer engagement activities.


## Success Metrics

The primary objective is to understand whether occasion-based strategic gifting can generate meaningful engagement with prospective customers and strengthen existing business relationships.

### Acquisition & Conversion

**Primary KPIs:**
- Meetings booked following gifting outreach
- Demo requests generated from gifting recipients

**Supporting metrics:**
- Gift delivery rate
- Recipient response rate
- Positive responses or expressions of interest
- Cost per meeting booked
- Cost per demo request

Sales opportunity progression and closed-won revenue may be monitored as secondary business outcomes, but they are not the primary measures of this marketing experiment.

### Retention

For existing customers, success would be measured differently, focusing on relationship engagement rather than immediate conversion.

Potential indicators include:
- Customer responses and feedback
- Referral introductions
- Engagement following milestone gifting
- Customer participation in follow-up activities

### Measurement Approach

Where possible, gifting activities should be tracked by occasion, recipient segment and campaign.

This would help identify which gifting moments generate the strongest response and whether the outcomes justify the investment.

As the initiative has not yet launched, these metrics represent the proposed measurement framework rather than actual results.
