
# System 001: Carrier Intelligence & Monitoring

**Area:** Marketing Operations & Product Marketing  
**Focus:** Information Management, Competitive Intelligence & Workflow Automation  
**Status:** Partially Implemented — Automation Proposed

## Context

The need for more consistent carrier intelligence monitoring was raised internally by the Content and Product Marketing teams.

Relevant carrier updates, including product announcements, service changes and operational developments, were distributed across multiple websites, newsletters and other communication channels.

Without a centralised approach, gathering this information required manual research and made it harder for the team to consistently stay informed.

I supported the development of a more structured monitoring process, helping identify relevant information sources, organise them into a centralised reference and explore opportunities for automation.

## Problem

How can carrier updates be monitored and organised more consistently without relying entirely on ad hoc manual research?

## Approach

The proposed solution combines existing information sources with a centralised tracking system and opportunities for automation.

## My Contribution

My role combined hands-on execution with input into how the monitoring process could be structured and improved.

Key contributions included:

- Researching and mapping relevant carrier information sources.
- Identifying newsletter subscriptions, announcement pages and available RSS feeds.
- Building an evergreen spreadsheet to centralise monitoring sources.
- Supporting the setup of recurring information collection through newsletter subscriptions.
- Exploring how Zapier and Slack could automate the distribution of carrier updates.
- Helping develop a repeatable approach that could be maintained and expanded over time.

### 1. Source Mapping

Identify relevant information channels for each carrier, including:

- Official newsletters and email subscriptions
- Carrier announcement and news pages
- RSS feeds, where available
- Other publicly accessible update channels

### 2. Centralised Source Register

Create an evergreen spreadsheet documenting relevant carrier information sources.

The register serves as a reference for:

- Which carriers are being monitored
- Where their updates are published
- Which subscription or monitoring methods are available
- How the team can access relevant information

### 3. Newsletter Subscriptions

Subscribe relevant team inboxes to available carrier newsletters to establish a recurring flow of information.

This provides an initial monitoring mechanism without requiring a new automation system.

### 4. Proposed RSS Automation

Explore an automated workflow connecting carrier RSS feeds to an internal communication channel using tools such as Zapier and Slack.

The proposed workflow would:

1. Detect new updates from selected RSS feeds.
2. Trigger an automation when new content is published.
3. Share relevant updates in a designated Slack channel.
4. Allow the team to review updates without manually checking individual websites.

This automation remains a proposed extension and has not yet been implemented.

## Current Status

**Implemented:**
- Developed a centralised carrier source register.
- Identified relevant information channels and subscription opportunities.
- Established newsletter subscriptions for recurring carrier updates.

**Proposed:**
- RSS-based monitoring automation.
- Automated distribution of carrier updates through Slack.
- Further refinement of monitoring coverage and workflow efficiency.

## Intended Outcome

The system aims to:

- Reduce the risk of missing important carrier announcements.
- Make information sources easier to locate and maintain.
- Reduce duplicated manual research.
- Support more consistent access to carrier intelligence.
- Establish a foundation for future monitoring automation.

## Results & Learnings

**Status:** Implementation ongoing.

The initial source register and newsletter subscriptions have been established, but the broader monitoring system has not yet been fully automated or formally evaluated.

Future evaluation could examine monitoring coverage, time spent gathering updates and the usefulness of information shared with the team.
